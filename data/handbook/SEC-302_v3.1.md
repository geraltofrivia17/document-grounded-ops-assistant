# Data Retention Schedule

## 1. Purpose

Keeping information longer than necessary increases risk and storage cost, and can break data protection law. This schedule sets how long each type of record is kept. At the end of the retention period, records are deleted or securely destroyed.

## 2. Operational records

| Record | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Delivery and proof-of-delivery records | 6 years from the delivery date |
| Vehicle telematics data | 2 years |
| CCTV footage from depots | 31 days, unless needed for an investigation |
| IT incident and change tickets | 3 years from closure |
| System access logs | 12 months |

## 3. People records

| Record | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Payroll records | 6 years after the end of the tax year they relate to |
| Personnel files for leavers | 6 years after the leaving date |
| Applications from unsuccessful candidates | 6 months after the recruitment decision |
| Accident book entries | 3 years from the date of the entry |

## 4. Legal holds

If records may be needed for a legal claim or investigation, the Information Security team places a legal hold. Records under a legal hold must not be deleted, even if their retention period has ended.

## 5. Questions

Ask the Information Security team before deleting any record type not listed in this schedule.
