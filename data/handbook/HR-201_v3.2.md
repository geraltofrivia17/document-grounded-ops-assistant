# Expenses Policy

## 1. Principles

Fernhollow reimburses reasonable costs that staff incur while doing their job.

## 2. Submitting a claim

Submit claims through the expenses system within 90 days of the date the cost was incurred.

## 3. Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every claim over £25.

## 4. Mileage

Staff who use their own car for business journeys can claim 40p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year and 25p per mile after that.

## 5. Meals and subsistence

When staying away overnight on business, you can claim up to £25 per night for an evening meal.

## 6. Approval

Your line manager approves claims up to £1,000. Claims over £1,000 also need approval from the budget holder.
