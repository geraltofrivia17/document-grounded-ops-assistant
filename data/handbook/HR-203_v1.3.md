# Remote and Hybrid Working

## 1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to office-based roles at the head office and depot offices. Warehouse, transport and site maintenance roles are site-based and are not eligible for hybrid working.

## 2. Hybrid pattern

Staff in hybrid roles work at least 2 days a week in the office. Teams agree their office days with their line manager, and the pattern is recorded in the HR system.

## 3. Working environment

Your home workspace must be safe and allow you to take calls without being overheard. Complete the home working self-assessment in the HR system within your first month of hybrid working.

## 4. Information security at home

Lock your screen whenever you leave your laptop. Do not print Confidential or Restricted documents at home. Follow SEC-301 for how information must be handled.

## 5. Working from abroad

You may work from outside the UK for up to 10 working days in a calendar year with approval from your line manager. Ask the Service Desk to check your laptop before you go. Working abroad for longer is not allowed, because of tax and employment law.

## 6. Home office equipment

The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a headset for hybrid workers. Laptops for hybrid workers are replaced every 3 years. If you leave Fernhollow, return all company equipment, including your laptop, within 10 working days of your last working day.
