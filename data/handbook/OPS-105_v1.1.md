# IT Equipment Issue and Return

## 1. Scope

This document covers company laptops, mobile phones, handheld scanners and accessories issued to staff and contractors.

## 2. Issuing equipment

New starters receive their laptop and phone from the Service Desk on their first day. The line manager must order equipment at least 10 working days before the start date. Each item is recorded against the person's name with its asset tag.

## 3. Looking after equipment

Keep equipment with you or locked away. Never leave a laptop visible in a vehicle. Do not install software that has not been approved by IT.

## 4. Lost or stolen equipment

Report lost or stolen equipment to the Service Desk on extension 4400 within 24 hours. If the device may contain Confidential or Restricted information, also report it as a security incident under SEC-303. Theft should also be reported to the police, and the crime reference number added to the ticket.

## 5. Returning equipment when you leave

Leavers must return their laptop, phone and accessories within 5 working days of their last working day. Equipment can be handed in at any depot reception or returned using a prepaid courier bag requested from the Service Desk. Equipment that is not returned may be charged at its replacement value.

## 6. Replacement cycle

Laptops are replaced every 4 years and phones every 3 years, or earlier if they fail.
