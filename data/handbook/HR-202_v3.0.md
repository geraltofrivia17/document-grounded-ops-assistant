# Business Travel Booking

## 1. Scope

This document covers rail travel, hotels and flights for business trips. Mileage and meals are covered by the Expenses Policy, HR-201.

## 2. Booking

Book all rail, hotel and flight travel through the company travel portal at least 10 days before the trip.

## 3. Rail travel

Travel standard class on all journeys. First class is not allowed.

## 4. Hotels

The maximum hotel rate is £120 per night outside London and £175 per night in London, including breakfast.

## 5. Flights

Domestic flights are not allowed. All flights abroad are economy class.

## 6. Travel abroad

Trips outside the UK need approval from a director before booking.
