# Phishing and Security Incident Reporting

## 1. What to report

Report anything that might put Fernhollow systems or information at risk: suspicious emails, texts or calls, lost devices, information sent to the wrong person, and unusual behaviour on your laptop.

## 2. Suspicious emails

Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook to report a suspicious email. If the button is not available, forward the email as an attachment to security@fernhollow.example. Do not delete the email until the Information Security team has reviewed it, and do not forward it to colleagues to ask their opinion.

## 3. If you clicked a link or entered your password

Call the Service Desk on extension 4400 straight away; outside Service Desk hours, call the out-of-hours line on extension 4499. Then change your password from a different, trusted device. Do not wait to see whether anything bad happens.

## 4. Information sent to the wrong person

If personal data or Confidential information is sent to the wrong person, report it as a security incident within 1 hour of noticing. The Information Security team decides whether the incident must be reported to the Information Commissioner's Office.

## 5. No blame

Staff who report an incident promptly will not be disciplined for the mistake that caused it. Late reporting makes incidents much harder to contain.
