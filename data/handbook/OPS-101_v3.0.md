# Incident Management Procedure

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure explains how Fernhollow Distribution staff report, prioritise and resolve IT and operational incidents. It applies to all depots, the head office and remote workers. An incident is any unplanned interruption to a service, or a reduction in its quality, including failures of the warehouse management system, handheld scanners, label printers, vehicle telematics and office systems.

## 2. Reporting an incident

Report incidents to the IT Service Desk through the service portal or by phone on extension 4400. The Service Desk is staffed from 07:00 to 19:00, Monday to Saturday. Outside these hours, call the out-of-hours line on extension 4499, which pages the on-call engineer described in OPS-103.

When you report an incident, give the site, the system affected, the time the problem started, how many people or vehicles are affected and any error message shown.

## 3. Priority levels

Every incident is given a priority by the Service Desk when it is logged.

- **P1 (critical):** a whole depot cannot pick, pack or dispatch, or a system failure is stopping vehicles leaving. Also any suspected data breach.
- **P2 (high):** a service is badly degraded at one site, or a single critical system has failed but a workaround exists.
- **P3 (moderate):** one team or a small group of users is affected and a workaround exists.
- **P4 (low):** a single user is affected, or the request is cosmetic.

## 4. Response and resolution targets

| Priority | Acknowledge within | Update the reporter every | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | 15 minutes | 30 minutes | 4 hours |
| P2 | 30 minutes | 2 hours | 8 working hours |
| P3 | 4 working hours | 1 working day | 3 working days |
| P4 | 1 working day | 3 working days | 10 working days |

Acknowledgement means a named engineer has accepted the ticket and contacted the reporter. The clock runs 24 hours a day for P1 and P2 incidents. For P3 and P4 incidents it runs only during Service Desk hours.

## 5. Major incidents

A P1 incident is declared a major incident by the Duty Operations Manager. When a major incident is declared, the Service Desk opens a bridge call within 15 minutes and invites the on-call engineer, the affected site manager and the system owner. The Duty Operations Manager sends a status update to the operations leadership group every 30 minutes until the service is restored.

## 6. Closing an incident

An incident is closed only when the reporter confirms the service is working, or after two unsuccessful attempts to contact the reporter over two working days.

## 7. Post-incident review

Every P1 incident needs a post-incident review. The review meeting must take place within 5 working days of the service being restored. The system owner writes the review, which records the timeline, the root cause, what went well and the actions agreed, each with an owner and a due date. Reviews are stored with the incident ticket.
