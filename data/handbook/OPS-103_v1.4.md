# On-Call and Out-of-Hours Support

## 1. Purpose

Depots dispatch vehicles from 04:00, so some systems must be supported outside Service Desk hours. This document explains how the on-call rota works and what is expected of on-call engineers.

## 2. The rota

The on-call rota runs for one week at a time. Handover takes place every Monday at 09:00, when the outgoing engineer briefs the incoming engineer on any open incidents. Engineers can swap rota weeks with a colleague if their line manager agrees and the swap is recorded on the rota at least 48 hours in advance.

## 3. Being paged

Calls to the out-of-hours line on extension 4499 page the on-call engineer. The on-call engineer must respond to a page within 20 minutes by calling back the person who raised it. If the engineer does not respond within 20 minutes, the page is escalated to the IT Operations Manager.

## 4. Fitness to respond

While on call, engineers must be able to reach a laptop and a reliable internet connection within 20 minutes, and must not drink alcohol.

## 5. Payment

On-call engineers receive an availability payment of £45 for each weekday night and £70 for each Saturday or Sunday. Time spent working on a call-out is paid at time and a half, with a minimum of one hour for each call-out.

## 6. Rest after a night call-out

If an engineer works for more than 3 hours between 00:00 and 06:00, they may start work up to 4 hours later than usual the next day without using annual leave. They should tell their line manager before their normal start time.
