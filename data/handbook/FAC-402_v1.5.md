# Fire Evacuation and Emergency Procedure

## 1. If you discover a fire

Raise the alarm by breaking the glass at the nearest fire alarm call point. Call 999 if it is safe to do so. Only use a fire extinguisher if you have been trained, the fire is small and you have a clear way out.

## 2. When the alarm sounds

Stop work immediately and leave by the nearest safe exit. Do not use lifts and do not stop to collect belongings. Forklift and reach truck drivers must lower their forks, switch off and leave the truck clear of exits.

## 3. Assembly points

Go to the assembly point shown on the site plan by each exit. At Ashby Park depot this is the staff car park by the main gate. At Calder Wharf depot it is the grass area beside the visitor car park.

## 4. Roll call

Shift supervisors take a roll call at the assembly point using the electronic sign-in list, and report anyone missing to the fire warden in charge. Hosts are responsible for their visitors.

## 5. Fire wardens

Each site has at least one trained fire warden on every shift. Fire wardens sweep their area, if safe, and report to the warden in charge. Nobody may re-enter the building until the fire service or the warden in charge says it is safe.

## 6. Drills and testing

Fire alarms are tested weekly. Full evacuation drills are held twice a year at each site, including at least one drill on a night shift.
