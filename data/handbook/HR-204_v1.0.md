# Annual Leave and Absence Reporting

## 1. Annual leave

The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Full-time staff receive 25 days of annual leave plus bank holidays. Part-time staff receive a pro-rata allowance.

## 2. Requesting leave

Request leave in the HR system. Give notice of at least twice the length of the leave you are requesting; for example, give two weeks' notice for one week of leave. Warehouse and transport staff cannot take leave in December without the site manager's agreement, because of peak trading.

## 3. Carrying leave over

You can carry up to 5 days of unused leave into the next leave year. Carried-over leave must be used by 30 June, or it is lost.

## 4. Reporting sickness

If you are unwell and cannot work, phone your line manager before your shift starts, or no later than 1 hour after your usual start time. A text or email is not enough unless your line manager has agreed it in advance. Keep your manager updated on each day of absence unless you have a fit note.

## 5. Evidence of sickness

For absences of up to 7 calendar days, complete a self-certification form in the HR system when you return. For absences of more than 7 calendar days, you need a fit note from a doctor or another authorised healthcare professional.

## 6. Return to work

After any period of sickness absence, your line manager will hold a short return-to-work conversation on your first day back.
