# Incident Management Procedure

## 1. Purpose and scope

This procedure explains how Fernhollow Distribution staff report, prioritise and resolve IT incidents. It applies to all depots and the head office.

## 2. Reporting an incident

Report incidents to the IT Service Desk through the service portal or by phone on extension 4400. The Service Desk is staffed from 08:00 to 18:00, Monday to Friday. Outside these hours, call the out-of-hours line on extension 4499.

## 3. Priority levels

- **P1 (critical):** a whole depot cannot dispatch.
- **P2 (high):** a service is badly degraded at one site.
- **P3 (moderate):** one team is affected and a workaround exists.
- **P4 (low):** a single user is affected.

## 4. Response and resolution targets

| Priority | Acknowledge within | Target resolution |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | 30 minutes | 6 hours |
| P2 | 1 hour | 12 working hours |
| P3 | 1 working day | 5 working days |
| P4 | 2 working days | 15 working days |

## 5. Post-incident review

Every P1 incident needs a post-incident review within 10 working days of the service being restored.
