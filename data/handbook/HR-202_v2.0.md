# Business Travel Booking

## 1. Scope

This document covers rail travel, hotels and flights for business trips. Mileage and meals are covered by the Expenses Policy, HR-201.

## 2. Booking

Book all rail, hotel and flight travel through the company travel portal at least 7 days before the trip, so that cheaper fares are available. If you book through the portal, the cost is charged to the company and you do not need to claim it back.

## 3. Rail travel

Travel standard class. First class is allowed only when a single journey is scheduled to take more than 3 hours, and your line manager has agreed in advance.

## 4. Hotels

The maximum hotel rate is £110 per night outside London and £160 per night in London, including breakfast. If no hotel is available within the limit, ask the Finance team to approve a higher rate before booking.

## 5. Flights

Domestic flights are allowed only when the equivalent rail journey would take more than 5 hours each way. All flights are economy class.

## 6. Travel abroad

Trips outside the UK need approval from a director before booking. Check the travel advice for the destination and tell the Service Desk before you travel so that your laptop and phone will work abroad.
