# Site Visitor and Contractor Access

## 1. Visitors

All visitors sign in at reception, show photo identification and receive a visitor badge. Visitors must be escorted by their host in all operational areas, including the warehouse floor and the yard. Hosts must register expected visitors in the visitor system at least 1 working day in advance.

## 2. Contractors

Contractors must complete the site safety induction before starting work. The induction is valid for 12 months at the site where it was completed. Contractors carrying out hot work, work at height or work on electrical systems need a permit to work issued by the site manager.

## 3. Personal protective equipment

High-visibility clothing and safety footwear must be worn by everyone in the warehouse and yard. Reception keeps spare high-visibility vests for visitors.

## 4. Vehicles in the yard

The speed limit in all depot yards is 5 mph. Pedestrians must use marked walkways. Visiting drivers must stay in the drivers' waiting area while their vehicle is loaded or unloaded.

## 5. CCTV

All depots are covered by CCTV for safety and security. Footage is kept for 90 days and can be reviewed by the site manager after an incident.
