# Supplier Onboarding and Payment

## 1. When this applies

Follow this procedure before placing a first order with any new supplier of goods or services, including hauliers and subcontractors.

## 2. Due diligence

New suppliers must provide proof of public liability insurance of at least £5 million and, where they employ staff, employer's liability insurance. Suppliers that will process personal data on Fernhollow's behalf must also complete the data protection questionnaire, which Information Security reviews.

## 3. Approval

The Procurement team approves new suppliers once due diligence is complete. Approval usually takes 10 working days from receiving all documents. Orders must not be placed with a supplier until it has been approved and set up in the finance system.

## 4. Purchase orders

Every order must have a purchase order raised in the finance system before the goods or services are delivered. Invoices without a valid purchase order number will not be paid; they are returned to the supplier.

## 5. Payment terms

Approved invoices are paid within 45 days of the invoice date. Faster payment terms need written approval from the Finance Director.

## 6. Changes to bank details

Requests to change a supplier's bank details must be confirmed by phoning the supplier on a number already held in the finance system, never a number given in the request. This protects against invoice fraud.
