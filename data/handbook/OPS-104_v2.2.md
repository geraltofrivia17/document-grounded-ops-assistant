# Access Request and Revocation Procedure

## 1. Purpose

Access to Fernhollow systems is granted on the principle of least privilege: people get the access their role needs and no more. This procedure covers requesting, approving, reviewing and removing access.

## 2. Requesting access

Request access through the service portal using the "System access" form. Requests are fulfilled within 2 working days of the final approval. Do not share your login with a colleague who is waiting for access.

## 3. Approvals

Standard access needs approval from the requester's line manager. Privileged access, such as administrator rights, finance approval roles or access to the payroll system, also needs approval from the system owner. Contractors need an end date on every access request, which can be no more than 3 months ahead; access can be renewed.

## 4. Changing role

When someone moves to a new role, their line manager must request the access the new role needs and ask for access from the old role to be removed within 5 working days of the move.

## 5. Leavers

The line manager must submit a leaver request at least 5 working days before the person's last working day. All access for a leaver is removed by 17:00 on their last working day. If someone is dismissed or leaves without notice, the line manager must call the Service Desk on extension 4400 so that access is removed immediately.

## 6. Access reviews

System owners review who has access to their systems every quarter, in January, April, July and October. Any access that cannot be justified is removed within 5 working days of the review.
