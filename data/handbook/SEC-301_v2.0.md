# Information Classification and Handling

## 1. Why classification matters

Labelling information tells everyone how carefully it must be handled. The person who creates a document is responsible for classifying it.

## 2. Classification levels

- **Public:** approved for release outside the company, such as published price lists and job adverts.
- **Internal:** for Fernhollow staff and contractors only, such as procedures and team rotas. This is the default level.
- **Confidential:** information that could harm the company or customers if disclosed, such as customer contracts, delivery schedules for named customers and supplier pricing.
- **Restricted:** the most sensitive information, such as payroll data, staff health records, disciplinary records and security configurations.

## 3. Handling rules

Confidential and Restricted information must only be stored in approved company systems. Restricted information must be encrypted in transit and at rest, and shared only with named people who need it.

## 4. Email

Never send Confidential or Restricted information to a personal email account, including your own. When emailing Restricted information outside the company, use the "Encrypt" option in Outlook.

## 5. Removable media

USB drives and other removable media are blocked on company laptops. Only encrypted USB drives issued by IT may be used, and only for Internal or Public information.

## 6. Disposal

Put printed Confidential and Restricted documents in the locked confidential waste bins at every site. Never put them in ordinary recycling.
