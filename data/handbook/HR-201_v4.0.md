# Expenses Policy

## 1. Principles

Fernhollow reimburses reasonable costs that staff incur while doing their job. Claims must be for genuine business expenses, supported by evidence, and approved before payment.

## 2. Submitting a claim

Submit claims through the expenses system within 60 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims submitted later will not be paid unless the Finance Director agrees there were exceptional circumstances. Claims are paid with the next monthly payroll after approval.

## 3. Receipts

An itemised receipt is required for every claim over £10. A card statement alone is not accepted as a receipt. If a receipt is lost, complete a missing receipt declaration, which your line manager must sign.

## 4. Mileage

Staff who use their own car for business journeys can claim 45p per mile for the first 10,000 business miles in a tax year and 25p per mile after that. Your normal commute between home and your usual workplace cannot be claimed. Record the start and end point and the purpose of each journey.

## 5. Meals and subsistence

When staying away overnight on business, you can claim up to £25 per night for an evening meal. Alcohol cannot be claimed. For day trips lasting more than 5 hours away from your usual workplace, you can claim up to £8 for lunch.

## 6. Approval

Your line manager approves claims up to £500. Claims over £500 also need approval from the budget holder for the cost centre. Nobody may approve their own claim.
